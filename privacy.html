Privacy Policy

BRAIN is a private desktop application used only by its owner, Radu Alexandru Nicolae, to manage the owner's own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts (for example MisterX, https://www.youtube.com/@mister369x). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed — YouTube: through the YouTube API Services, BRAIN accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels. It uploads videos approved by the owner, sets their thumbnails, and reads each channel's basic information and video statistics.

Data accessed — TikTok: through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API, BRAIN reads only the basic profile of the owner's own TikTok accounts (display name and avatar) and sends videos approved by the owner to those accounts. It does not read followers, messages or any other user's data.

Storage: OAuth tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted with the operating system's protection. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party creators: for clipping, BRAIN stores the public channel link, business email and email conversation of creators who were contacted, only to request and record their permission. This data is never shared and is deleted on the creator's request.

Data deletion: when access is revoked, or on request to neuraledgeinstitutional@gmail.com, BRAIN deletes the stored tokens and all YouTube and TikTok data it holds within 7 days. Statistics read from the YouTube API are refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Revoking access: YouTube access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. BRAIN uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
